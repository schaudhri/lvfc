# Impeccable

Design context for the Lahore Virgil Football Club site. Read this before any
design or UX work — it records intent that the code cannot tell you.

## Design Context

### Users

**Primary: parents of children aged 2–18 in Lahore**, deciding whether to trust a
football academy with their child. They arrive from Instagram, WhatsApp or word
of mouth, usually on a phone, often mid-decision between LVFC and a local
alternative. The job to be done is *"work out whether this club is safe, serious
and near me — then get my child a place."*

Secondary audiences, both much smaller: **visiting clubs and partners** (league
registration, sponsorship) and **prospective coaches**.

The primary conversion is **Book A Spot** — registration on the club's Odoo
portal at `virgil-sports.odoo.com/programmes`. Every page should keep that
action reachable, and the site's job ends at handing the parent to the portal.

### Brand Personality

**Trustworthy, safe, credible.** A parent's first ten seconds should produce
reassurance, not excitement. Ambition and match-day energy are real parts of the
club, but they are the *second* note — the first is duty of care.

This is why the site publishes its safeguarding policy in full, names its
Designated Safeguarding Lead and Club Welfare Officer, and refuses to state a
fact no client document supports. That restraint is a brand asset, not
timidity — copy that overclaims would undermine the one thing the audience is
actually shopping for.

Voice: plain, specific, parental. Say "we don't have that yet" rather than
inventing it. No hype, no unverified numbers.

### Aesthetic Direction

Locked brand assets, from the club's guidelines:

| Token | Value | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `brand-maroon` | `#530E16` | Headers, filter pills, all headings |
| `brand-champagne` | `#B19855` | Every solid CTA, site-wide |
| `neutral-darkest` | `#130101` | Footer, stats band, media cards |
| `scheme-background` | `#EEEEEE` | Page ground |
| `--font-heading` | Tiller | Display |
| `--font-body` | Futura PT | Body and UI |

These are distinctive and licensed — Tiller and Futura PT are a genuine asset,
not a default. They are open to critique, but replacing them needs a real
argument, not a preference.

**Light mode only.** Dark mode is not planned; absent dark variants are not a
defect.

**Anti-reference: the generic template.** The build sits on Relume section
components, and the risk is that it reads as stock rather than as LVFC.
Identikit card grids, placeholder imagery and interchangeable section rhythm are
failures here even when they are technically correct.

### Design Principles

1. **Reassure before you sell.** Safeguarding, qualified coaches and real
   contact details outrank persuasion. If a section can only be made compelling
   by overclaiming, cut it.
2. **Never invent a fact.** Absent content shows an honest empty state — an
   `awaiting` resource card, a "still being written" article — never a dead
   link, a fake number or invented copy. This is already load-bearing across
   `data/resources.ts`, `data/blog.ts` and `data/programmes.ts`.
3. **Every page keeps Book A Spot within reach**, and it is always the same
   label and the same champagne button. One action, one name, one colour.
4. **Phone first.** The audience is on mobile, on Pakistani networks. Weight,
   touch targets and image payload are UX decisions, not engineering details.
5. **Earn the brand.** Tiller, maroon and champagne are what stop this looking
   like every other academy site. Use them with intent rather than letting
   Relume defaults set the tone.
